CHAPTER I

Origin Story

How it started

Every origin story needs an inciting incident. Mine was a broken script that refused to run at 2 a.m. — and the stubborn refusal to close the laptop until it did. That night turned into a career built on solving hard problems methodically, shipping software that holds up under pressure, and treating every bug as a plot twist worth chasing down.

Since then the mission has stayed the same: write code that's clean enough to read, fast enough to trust, and built to survive contact with real users. No capes required — just good architecture, sharper debugging instincts, and a refusal to leave things half-finished.
